
iLocate Hospital Dashboard & Reporting Requirements Specification
ROI-Focused Dashboard & Reporting System
For Motion-Based Accelerometer Asset Tracking
1. Purpose
The iLocate dashboard and reporting platform shall provide hospitals with measurable, quantifiable, and empirical operational intelligence demonstrating the value, utilization, efficiency, and ROI of the iLocate asset tracking system.

Because iLocate accelerometer tags only transmit while assets are in motion, the platform shall leverage movement intelligence, last-seen activity, location transitions, and utilization behavior rather than continuous telemetry.

The dashboard must help hospital leadership answer:

Are we reducing time spent searching for equipment?

Are assets being utilized efficiently?

Are departments hoarding equipment?

Can we reduce unnecessary equipment purchases?

Are workflow bottlenecks visible?

Is equipment distribution improving patient care efficiency?

What measurable ROI is the hospital receiving?

2. Dashboard Objectives
The dashboard shall:

Provide real-time operational visibility

16. Key Differentiator Requirements
Because iLocate uses motion-triggered accelerometer telemetry instead of continuous beacon transmission, the platform shall emphasize:

Operational movement intelligence

Utilization-based analytics

Availability prediction

Workflow optimization

Cost avoidance

Asset circulation intelligence

rather than precise real-time positioning.

This shall reduce infrastructure costs while still providing actionable operational value and measurable ROI.
